With 𝒵ν⁡(z) defined as in §10.25(ii),

10.29.1

𝒵ν−1⁡(z)−𝒵ν+1⁡(z)
=(2⁢ν/z)⁢𝒵ν⁡(z),

𝒵ν−1⁡(z)+𝒵ν+1⁡(z)
=2⁢𝒵ν′⁡(z).

ⓘ

Symbols:

𝒵ν⁡(z): modified cylinder function,
z: complex variable and
ν: complex parameter

For k=0,1,2,…,

10.29.4

(1z⁢ddz)k⁡(zν⁢𝒵ν⁡(z))
=zν−k⁢𝒵ν−k⁡(z),

(1z⁢ddz)k⁡(z−ν⁢𝒵ν⁡(z))
=z−ν−k⁢𝒵ν+k⁡(z).

ⓘ

Symbols:

dfdx: derivative of f with respect to x,
𝒵ν⁡(z): modified cylinder function,
k: nonnegative integer,
z: complex variable and
ν: complex parameter
