When ν is fixed and z→∞,

10.7.8

Jν⁡(z)
=2/(π⁢z)⁢(cos⁡(z−12⁢ν⁢π−14⁢π)+e|ℑ⁡z|⁢o⁡(1)),

Yν⁡(z)
=2/(π⁢z)⁢(sin⁡(z−12⁢ν⁢π−14⁢π)+e|ℑ⁡z|⁢o⁡(1)),

|ph⁡z|≤π−δ(<π).

ⓘ

Symbols:

Jν⁡(z): Bessel function of the first kind,
Yν⁡(z): Bessel function of the second kind,
π: the ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter,
cos⁡z: cosine function,
e: base of natural logarithm,
ℑ⁡: imaginary part,
o⁡(x): order less than,
ph: phase,
sin⁡z: sine function,
z: complex variable,
ν: complex parameter and
δ: small positive constant
