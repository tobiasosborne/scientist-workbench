§10.28 Wronskians and Cross-Products

ⓘ

Keywords:

Wronskians, cross-products, modified Bessel functions

Notes:
For the Wronskians use (1.13.5) and the limiting forms in
§10.30. For the cross-products apply (10.29.2).

10.28.1

𝒲⁡{Iν⁡(z),I−ν⁡(z)}=Iν⁡(z)⁢I−ν−1⁡(z)−Iν+1⁡(z)⁢I−ν⁡(z)=−2⁢sin⁡(ν⁢π)/(π⁢z),

ⓘ

Symbols:

𝒲: Wronskian,
π: the ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter,
Iν⁡(z): modified Bessel function of the first kind,
sin⁡z: sine function,
z: complex variable and
ν: complex parameter
