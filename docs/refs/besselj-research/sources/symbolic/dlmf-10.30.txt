When ν is fixed and z→0,

10.30.1

Iν⁡(z)∼(12⁢z)ν/Γ⁡(ν+1),

ν≠−1,−2,−3,…,

ⓘ

Symbols:

Γ⁡(z): gamma function,
∼: asymptotic equality,
Iν⁡(z): modified Bessel function of the first kind,
z: complex variable and
ν: complex parameter

When ν is fixed and z→∞,

10.30.4

Iν⁡(z)
∼ez/2⁢π⁢z,

|ph⁡z|≤12⁢π−δ,

ⓘ

Symbols:

∼: asymptotic equality,
π: the ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter,
e: base of natural logarithm,
Iν⁡(z): modified Bessel function of the first kind,
ph: phase,
z: complex variable,
ν: complex parameter and
δ: small positive constant
