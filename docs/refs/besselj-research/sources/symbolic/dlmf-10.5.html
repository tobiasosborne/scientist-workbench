§10.5 Wronskians and Cross-Products

ⓘ
Keywords:
Bessel functions, Hankel functions, Wronskians, cross-product, cross-products
Notes:
For the Wronskians use (1.13.5) and the limiting forms in §10.7. Then for the cross-products apply (10.6.2).
