When ν is fixed and z→∞,

10.7.8 Jν⁡(z) =2/(π⁢z)⁢(cos⁡(z−12⁢ν⁢π−14⁢π)+e|ℑ⁡z|⁢o⁡(1)),
Yν⁡(z) =2/(π⁢z)⁢(sin⁡(z−12⁢ν⁢π−14⁢π)+e|ℑ⁡z|⁢o⁡(1)),
|ph⁡z|≤π−δ(<π).
