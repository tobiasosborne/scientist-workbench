§10.50 Wronskians and Cross-Products

ⓘ

Keywords:

Wronskians, cross-products, spherical Bessel functions

Notes:
That the Wronskians are constant multiples of z−2 follows from
(1.13.5). The constants can be found from the limiting forms
(and their derivatives) given in §§10.52(i) or 10.52(ii).
For (10.50.3) combine (10.50.1) with (10.51.1)
and (10.51.2). For (10.50.4) use
(10.49.2)–(10.49.5).

10.50.1

𝒲⁡{𝗃n⁡(z),𝗒n⁡(z)}
=z−2,

𝒲⁡{𝗁n(1)⁡(z),𝗁n(2)⁡(z)}
=−2⁢i⁢z−2.

ⓘ

Symbols:

𝒲: Wronskian,
i: imaginary unit,
𝗃n⁡(z): spherical Bessel function of the first kind,
𝗒n⁡(z): spherical Bessel function of the second kind,
𝗁n(1)⁡(z): spherical Bessel function of the third kind,
𝗁n(2)⁡(z): spherical Bessel function of the third kind,
n: integer and
z: complex variable
