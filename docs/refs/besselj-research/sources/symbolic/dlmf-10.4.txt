10.4.1

J−n⁡(z)
=(−1)n⁢Jn⁡(z),

Y−n⁡(z)
=(−1)n⁢Yn⁡(z),

ⓘ

Symbols:

Jν⁡(z): Bessel function of the first kind,
Yν⁡(z): Bessel function of the second kind,
n: integer and
z: complex variable

10.4.3

=Jν⁡(z)+i⁢Yν⁡(z),

Hν(2)⁡(z)
=Jν⁡(z)−i⁢Yν⁡(z),

Symbols:

Hν(2)⁡(z): Bessel function of the third kind (or Hankel function),
i: imaginary unit,
z: complex variable and

10.4.5

Jν⁡(z)=csc⁡(ν⁢π)⁢(Y−ν⁡(z)−Yν⁡(z)⁢cos⁡(ν⁢π)).

ⓘ

Symbols:

Jν⁡(z): Bessel function of the first kind,
Yν⁡(z): Bessel function of the second kind,
π: the ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter,
csc⁡z: cosecant function,
cos⁡z: cosine function,
z: complex variable and
ν: complex parameter
