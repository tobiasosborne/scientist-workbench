§10.38 Derivatives with Respect to Order

ⓘ

Keywords:

derivatives with respect to order, modified Bessel functions

Notes:
(10.38.1) is obtained by differentiation of
(10.25.2); compare (10.15.1). For (10.38.2)
use (10.27.4). (10.38.3)–(10.38.5) are
proved in a similar way to (10.15.3)–(10.15.5).
(10.38.6) and (10.38.7) are stated without proof and
in a slightly different notation in Magnus et al. (1966, §3.3.3).
Both cases of (10.38.6) can be derived by a method analogous to
that used for (10.15.6) and (10.15.7).
(10.38.7) follows from (10.38.2) and (10.38.6).
