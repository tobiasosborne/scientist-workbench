§10.5 Wronskians and Cross-Products

ⓘ

Keywords:

Bessel functions, Hankel functions, Wronskians, cross-product, cross-products

Notes:
For the Wronskians use (1.13.5) and the limiting forms in
§10.7. Then for the cross-products apply (10.6.2).

10.5.1

𝒲⁡{Jν⁡(z),J−ν⁡(z)}=Jν+1⁡(z)⁢J−ν⁡(z)+Jν⁡(z)⁢J−ν−1⁡(z)=−2⁢sin⁡(ν⁢π)/(π⁢z),

ⓘ

Symbols:

Jν⁡(z): Bessel function of the first kind,
𝒲: Wronskian,
π: the ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter,
sin⁡z: sine function,
z: complex variable and
ν: complex parameter
